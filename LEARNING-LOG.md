# Learning Log

Append every session. Teach-backs in your own words, quiz scores, and anything that
surprised you. `05-ACCOUNTABILITY.md` calls this the highest-value artifact in the
program — in Week 16 you mine it for interview answers, so write it for a reader who
is you in December.

Format per entry:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — W<n> <Weekday> <AM|PM>
**Teach-back:** <the concept, explained as if to a colleague, in your own words>
**Quiz:** <n>/<total>  <what you missed and why>
**Surprised me:** <optional — the thing that didn't match your prior model>
```
