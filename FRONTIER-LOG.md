# Frontier Log

Friday mornings, 30 minutes across the eight currency sources in
`program/04-RESOURCES.md`, then 30 minutes writing the week's entry here.

This is the artifact that answers "how do you stay current?" with evidence rather than
a list of newsletters. By December it is sixteen weeks of tracked platform movement in
your own words.

Format per entry:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Week <n> frontier watch
**Moved this week:** <what actually changed, with links>
**Changes my plan:** <or "nothing did" — say so plainly, that is a real finding>
**Worth a second look:** <parked items>
```
